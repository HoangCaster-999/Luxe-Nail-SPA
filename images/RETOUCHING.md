# Salon portfolio retouching

The 14 original photographs supplied by the owner are preserved in `Ảnh nail/`.
Website versions are in `retouched/`: full photographs for the gallery/lightbox and `-thumb.jpg` previews for the grid.

## Editing

Edited with the built-in Imagegen tool, then exported as quality-90 JPEGs with 300px-wide thumbnails. The ombre, crystal French and yellow/chocolate photographs are additionally cropped around the hands to remove distracting lower-body background. Responsive CSS crops the hero, introduction and ombre service image; the featured gallery and lightbox show the complete exported photograph.

Prompt applied separately to each photograph:

> Use case: lighting-weather. Edit target: attached REAL nail salon portfolio photograph. Primary request: professionally correct exposure and improve clarity of this exact photograph for a website. Lift dark midtones/shadows naturally with soft daylight, protect white highlights, neutralize excessive colour casts, subtle denoise and sharpening. Preserve EXACTLY all original nail art, colours, nail shapes and lengths, decorations, every finger, hand pose, jewellery, skin texture and original scene. No new manicure, no changed design, no added/removed gems or flowers, no skin whitening or plastic smoothing. Preserve photographic authenticity. Crop excess empty area above or below hands if useful while keeping EVERY originally visible nail completely visible. One portrait photograph, no collage, no text.

For `pearl-gold-french`, the same exposure/clarity instructions explicitly named the pearly French tips, pink base and gold outlines, with the original portrait framing retained.

AI retouching can subtly reinterpret tiny details. Keep the originals as the reference for exact designs and colours.

## Placement

- Hero and sharing image: pearlescent French tips with gold outlines.
- Introduction: burgundy bows, checks and dots.
- Introduction detail photographs: magenta flowers with gold details and white flowers with silver outlines.
- Ombre service and Full Set Ombre price entry: pink-to-white ombre with floral accents.
- French / White Tips: pearlescent French tips.
- Design: magenta flowers and gold details.
- Diamonds: crystal-bordered white French tips.
- Gallery: all 14 salon photographs, with descriptions of what is visible.

The Gallery appears before the treatment illustrations and advances every 4.5 seconds while visible. A full-width carousel shows the current image between previews of adjacent photographs, with a 650ms horizontal sliding transition and a compact thumbnail strip. Display crops fill their frames; the lightbox shows the complete exported photograph. Playback pauses for the lightbox, manual photo selection, keyboard interaction, hidden tabs and offscreen content. Visitors can pause/resume explicitly; reduced-motion preferences disable autoplay and transitions by default.

The source photographs do not establish acrylic versus BIAB application, infill procedures, gel removal, pedicures or children's services. A subsequent [treatment audit](treatments/README.md) records the individually checked service illustrations and new generated replacements. The gallery contains only the owner's supplied salon photographs.
